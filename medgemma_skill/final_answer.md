WHAT THE DIFFERENCE IS (read from the weights). The language-model delta is dense and flat: a 0.25 to 0.37 relative change in every projection of every layer, as full-rank as the base matrices (the top 256 of 2,560 directions hold 21 percent of an MLP delta's energy, the same as for the base weights), with no head-level or neuron-level concentration (heads within a layer differ by about 10 percent; the top tenth of neurons hold 11 to 12 percent of the energy). 28 percent of the attention-plus-MLP delta energy is a per-tensor rescaling of the base tensor (k and v scale together per layer by factors from 0.64 to 1.75, down_proj by 0.56 to 1.66) that Gemma 3's q/k norms and post-sublayer RMSNorms cancel, so it is functionally null: the apparent spikes in layers 7, 8, 27, 29 are this component, whole-tensor selections are scale-safe, and entry masks or magnitude trimming on these tensors would distort them. The vision tower is a retrained encoder, not a fine-tuned one: target tensors have 4 to 7 times the base norm with cosine near zero and chance-level singular-subspace overlap, so it is meaningful only as a block. The embedding delta is a uniform 26 percent change on nearly every row, largest on rare and unused tokens and smallest on frequent and structural tokens; it looks like drift but is co-adapted with the late layers through the tied output head.

WHAT THE PASSES SHOWED (pool items: 128 SLAKE images, 64 medqa, 64 instruction prompts; teacher-forced probe of 256 chats). Vision plus projector alone makes BASE's language model blind (SLAKE F1 0.18 against BASE 0.315, TARGET 0.535): it calls an MRI a CT scan. Image reading is in the language model and scales linearly with the number of early layers at full strength (share of the SLAKE gain 53 percent with none, 67 with layers 0-6, 83 with 0-16, 112 with all 34 behind BASE's embedding), and it sits in the MLPs: the MLPs of layers 0-16 alone at full strength give the same 83 percent as the whole layers, while the attention of layers 0-16 at full strength gives nothing (49 percent) and only adds general drift. TARGET's late layers require TARGET's embedding: all 34 layers with BASE's head, or layers 17-33 alone, loop and are cut at the 2,048-token limit in a third of answers; moving layers and embedding together at the same strength is coherent. A half-prefix (layers 0-16 at full, rest BASE) is incoherent on the general side (mmlu letter log-probability worse than both parents, further from BASE's general texts than TARGET itself). The uniform half-strength interpolation of the whole language model is a clean soup (50 percent of TARGET's shift from BASE's general and instruction texts, 78 percent of the medical-text agreement gain, no loops) but reads images only half (53 percent). Dropping the attention delta from the final recipe is incoherent again (medical letters fall from 86 to 65 percent of the gain, stop-token log-probabilities collapse), so attention must move with the MLPs at reduced strength. The evaluation's fractional-strength control, every tensor at 0.5 with the vision tower included, scores SLAKE F1 0.230, below BASE: half of a retrained encoder is neither encoder, which is why the vision block is at full strength in my selection and the LM is interpolated behind it.

WHY I EXPECT IT TO KEEP THE MEDICAL GAINS WITHOUT THE GENERAL REGRESSION. The imaging gain is carried by the encoder block plus the early MLPs, both at full strength, and reproduces 80 percent of TARGET's SLAKE gain on held-out images; the medical text gain is carried mostly by the same early MLPs and reaches TARGET's level on held-out questions. The parts that carry only drift relative to BASE (attention everywhere, late MLPs, the output head) are at half strength, which halves their contribution to the general shift while keeping the model coherent (no loops, instruction following and practice mmlu equal to BASE). The honest caveat is that the medical knowledge and the general drift of the language model live in the same early MLPs and could not be separated further by structure: the frontier between the uniform half-strength soup (53 percent imaging, 50 percent shift) and this selection (80 percent imaging, about 76 percent shift) is nearly linear, and I chose the higher-medical end because TARGET's absolute general regression on practice items is small (mmlu +3, instruction prompts -2 points) while its medical gains are large. Compared with the controls at matched size (99 percent of the parameters, which makes magnitude-trimmed, randomly dropped and random selections essentially TARGET), this selection gives up about a fifth of the imaging gain and nothing measurable on medical text in exchange for a coherent model that stays closer to BASE; compared with the fractional-strength control it keeps the imaging gain that the control destroys.
