The editor's task: the driver prompt of run t1, verbatim from the TASK section on. The harness preamble before it (the submit command and local paths) is omitted; local paths below are replaced by placeholders.

TASK. You have two instruction-tuned vision-language models of identical architecture (34 decoder layers of width 2560, 8 query and 4 key/value heads of size 256, gated MLP of width 10240, RMSNorm, vocabulary 262,208 with tied embeddings; a 27-layer SigLIP vision tower of width 1152 and a pooling projector to 256 image tokens): BASE, a general assistant, and TARGET, the same developer's medical variant. TARGET scores higher than BASE on medical imaging and clinical question answering and lower on general knowledge and instruction following. You are given the exact per-tensor difference DELTA = TARGET - BASE. Your job is to build EDITED = BASE + (your selection of DELTA): choose the parts of the difference that carry the medical gains and leave out the parts that carry the general regression, by READING THE WEIGHTS and reasoning about what each part does, confirmed with a limited number of forward passes. Selections can be whole tensors, components, layers, rows, columns, entry masks, or low-rank factors of a tensor's difference; you never write arbitrary weights.

HOW IT WILL BE MEASURED. After you finish, EDITED, BASE and TARGET are evaluated on radiology VQA (SLAKE, token F1), hard medical multiple choice (MedXpertQA text, 10 options), general-knowledge multiple choice (MMLU-Pro, 10 options) and instruction following (IFEval, prompt-level strict), with this fixed protocol: the chat template, no system prompt, sampling with temperature 1.0, top_k 64, top_p 0.95, at most 2,048 new tokens; for multiple choice the LAST "Final Answer: (X)" is graded; for VQA the first line is graded by token F1. You never see these benchmarks. Your result is reported as the share of TARGET's medical gain that EDITED keeps and the share of BASE's general score that EDITED recovers, next to IFEval and the share of answers cut at the token limit, and next to controls: the whole difference at fractional strength, the vision-tower difference alone, the language-model difference alone, magnitude-trimmed and randomly-dropped differences at your selection's parameter count, and a random selection of the same parameter count. To matter, your selection must beat those at matched size.

WHAT COUNTS. Selections that come from understanding the difference: which components and layers carry which capability, what the vision-tower change does versus the language-model change, whether a tensor's difference is low-rank or structured, which rows of the embedding difference are medical vocabulary, what the attention changes in specific layers do. Then confirm with forward passes on practice problems and keep what the evidence supports. Out of scope: anything that is not a selection of DELTA (the kernel refuses other writes); selecting everything (that is TARGET); choosing by a benchmark name you guessed rather than by evidence.

WHAT YOU HAVE.
1. A persistent Python kernel (no GPU, no internet): variables survive between code blocks, like a notebook. Predefined: NAMES, SHAPES[name], COMPONENT[name] (vision, projector, attention, mlp, norm, embeddings), LAYER[name], STATS (per-tensor base norm, delta norm, relative norm, changed share; roll-ups by component and by component x layer), load_base(name), load_target(name), delta(name) (float32), load(name) (the CURRENT edited tensor), select(name, mask=None, rows=None, cols=None, scale=1.0), select_lowrank(name, k, scale=1.0), select_component(component, layers=None, scale=1.0), select_layers(layers, components=None, scale=1.0), drop(name), drop_all(), selected(), selection_summary(), tok, CONFIG, eval_prompt(x), eval_prompt_ids(x), practice_items(), and the paths WORK, SELECTED, BASE_DIR, TARGET_DIR, DELTA_DIR, PRACTICE, POOL. Exact semantics, refusals and the printed line of each select call are in harness/README.md. Gradients are disabled. One execution may take up to 900 seconds and about 9 GB of arrays; output is cut at 10,000 characters. You may run code up to 300 times. If the kernel is restarted (time or memory limit), its variables are lost and you are told so. Read tensors one at a time; the models are 4.3 billion parameters each. The embedding delta (262,208 x 2560) may be selected whole or by rows (token ids).
2. Practice problems with answers (none in the evaluation), one JSON object per line in <data>/practice/practice_v2.jsonl: 2,000 USMLE-style 4-option questions (ids medqa_train_*), 2,000 Indian medical entrance 4-option questions (medmcqa_train_*), 1,000 general-knowledge 4-option questions from many non-medical subjects (mmlu_val_*), and 1,000 radiology visual questions with their images (slake_train_*, open and closed answers, graded by token F1 and exact match; the image attaches automatically when you list the id). The 512 ids in <data>/practice/pool_v2.txt (128 of each kind) have BASE and TARGET answers cached, so passes on them cost no GPU time for those two models; other ids work too. You may also write your own prompts (ungraded), including instruction-following prompts of your own design to watch for regression.
3. A forward-pass tool with a budget of 25 forward passes in total. A forward pass is one of two things, each counts as one pass, and a request that fails costs nothing:
   (a) GENERATION: reply with one block that starts with ```forward and contains JSON: {"type": "generate", "model": "edited" | "base" | "target" | "both", "problems": ["<practice id>", ...], "prompts": ["<your own user message>", ...], "n": 1, "max_tokens": 2048}. items x n <= 512; with "both" <= 256 per model. "both" runs EDITED and BASE on the same items with the same seeds and returns the paired comparison (accuracy difference edited - base over items with its standard error, samples wrong-to-right and right-to-wrong), the per-model accuracy by source, mean tokens, cut share and failure counts; texts, extracted answers and grades go to work/gen_<k>_edited.jsonl and work/gen_<k>_base.jsonl. EDITED is rebuilt from your current selection on every request (about 2 minutes) and generated live.
   (b) TEACHER-FORCED: {"type": "teacher_forced", "model": "edited" | "base" | "target", "texts": [...] or "chats": [[{"role": "user", "content": "..."}, {"role": "assistant", "content": "..."}], ...], "return": {"token_logprobs": true, "topk": 5, "hidden_states": [0, 10, 20, 34], "mlp_act": [10]}}. Up to 256 texts of up to 4,096 tokens, outputs up to 4 GB, written as .npy files to work/fwd_<k>/ with meta.json. This gives a dense signal on every token (log-probabilities, residual stream, MLP activations) for any text you choose, including practice questions with their reference answers.
   Full request grammar, limits and summary fields: harness/README.md.
4. A shell, as described in the preamble. The GPU is not visible to it and must not be made visible; loading or running any model anywhere except through the forward-pass tool is forbidden; so is reading any benchmark or results file. Every command you run is logged and audited after the run; a run that breaks these rules is discarded.

HOW TO WORK.
1. Read first. STATS gives per-tensor and per-component norms of DELTA; the vision tower, projector, attention, MLP and embedding differences have very different sizes and structures. Form hypotheses about which parts carry imaging gains, text-medical gains and general regression before spending passes.
2. Diagnose with a paired pass. One "both" generation on a mixed practice set (medical text, general, imaging) shows where BASE and TARGET differ (TARGET's cached answers are reported when you include "target" or ask for it) and what EDITED does under your first selection.
3. Localize causally. Test selections that differ in one thing (a component, a layer range, a rank), on the same problems, and look for dose-response. A part that is large is not therefore the part that matters.
4. Measure with enough power. Accuracy over n generations has a standard error of about 50/sqrt(n) points: 4.4 at 128, 2.2 at 512. Compare EDITED and BASE on the SAME problems with "both"; never compare accuracies measured on different problems; never repeat an identical request with an unchanged selection. Sampling at temperature 1.0 makes paired runs noisy even with shared seeds; treat differences inside two standard errors as noise. Keep a selection only if its medical gain is clear AND its general-practice score does not drop, on problems not used to choose it.
5. Plan passes: diagnostics, a few selection comparisons, a final confirmation on held-out practice problems you fixed in advance.
6. Work efficiently: state the hypothesis of each code block in its first comment line; load things once; print compact summaries, never whole arrays and never raw vocabulary lists; print short excerpts of generated texts, at most about 300 characters each; keep <repo>/runs/t1/work/notes.md current (findings with numbers, selections tried and their measured effect, the current best selection with its exact recipe, the problem sets you used); it is what you get back if your conversation is restarted.

EXPLAIN EVERY STEP. In every message, before your code or forward block, explain in plain English (3 to 8 sentences): what the last result showed, what you conclude, what you will do now and why, and what outcome would confirm or refute your hypothesis. In your final answer, summarize the path that led to your selection and what you learned about what each part of the difference does. These explanations are read by the experimenters and quoted in the write-up.

RULES. Not allowed: (a) gradient descent or any other training: no backward passes, autograd or optimizers, and no fitting of weights or masks to a loss or to activations, iterative or closed-form (summary statistics such as norms, covariances, correlations, PCA and SVD are allowed, and so are selections you compute from them with coefficients you choose and test); (b) running a model other than through the forward-pass tool; (c) internet access; (d) reading evaluation sets or results. Allowed: any analysis of DELTA and of forward-pass results, any use of the practice problems, any selection of DELTA the kernel accepts.

HOW THE SESSION RUNS. It continues until all 25 forward passes are used (a message without a fenced block does not end it earlier); after that, make your final choice (the selection in <repo>/runs/t1/work/selected when you finish is submitted) and send a final message without a fenced block, saying what you selected, how many parameters it changes, the evidence for it, and why you expect it to keep the medical gains without the general regression. Then reply to the experimenters with that same summary.
